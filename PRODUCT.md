# Krisha's Internship Portfolio

## Role & Goal
You are a student/aspiring professional applying to internships. This portfolio exists to showcase your work, skills, and learning in a memorable, personality-driven way that stands out to recruiters and hiring managers.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Warm earth-tone art deco with vintage/retro vibes.** Think: sophisticated, textured, ornamental—influenced by 1930s-40s design language but fresh. The palette is warm (golden yellows, deep browns, maroons, peachy tones, cream accents) with nature-inspired motifs (organic patterns, geometric abstraction, decorative flourishes). Texture and depth matter; this feels crafted, not corporate.

Color palette reference: Coffee, Maroon, Clay Dust, Creme, Leather Couch, Deep Peach, Guava, River Pine.

Material inspiration: Art deco tiles, embossed details, aged/weathered textures, ornamental borders.

## Content Structure

### 1. Hero / Landing
- Intro statement (who you are, what you do)
- Call-to-action to explore projects

### 2. About Me
- Personal introduction
- Skills/competencies
- A bit of personality

### 3. Projects Showcase
- Grid of project cards (icons + images)
- Each shows: project name + brief summary
- Click to expand → full project page

### 4. Project Detail Pages
- Project name & overview
- **Tech Stack**: technologies/tools used
- **Media**: embedded videos, screenshots, slides (carousel or gallery)
- **Links**: repo, live demo, or relevant URLs
- **Learnings**: key takeaways and what you gained

### 5. Contact & Resume
- Contact form or email link
- Resume download/link
- Social links (GitHub, LinkedIn, etc.)

## Success Criteria
- ✓ Portfolio is visually distinctive and matches the aesthetic (stands out vs. standard templates)
- ✓ Projects are scannable but deep on click (fast to skim, rich when explored)
- ✓ Personality shines through (warm, approachable, thoughtful—not generic)
- ✓ Works on mobile and desktop
- ✓ Fast load, smooth interactions
- ✓ Resume easily accessible
